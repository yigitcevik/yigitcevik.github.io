App Purpose & Functionality

The Cartel 3 - Mafia Tycoon is an immersive strategy and idle simulation mobile game developed by Yiğit Çevik. The primary purpose of the app is to provide users with an engaging entertainment experience where they can manage criminal operations, build business empires, engage in tactical PvP battles, and compete in global leaderboards.

Data Collection & Transparency

To provide core features such as authentication, account progression sync, and secure cloud storage, our app requests specific user data transparently:

  • Email Address & Profile Data: Used strictly for account identification, Google Play Games authentication, and saving player progress across sessions.
  • Analytics & Crash Reports: Collected via Firebase services to monitor app performance, stability, and fix technical bugs.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Utilized by Google AdMob to display relevant in-game rewards and advertisements.

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. For complete details, please review our Privacy Policy.
