Herd It!

Sheep Puzzle · by Sacred Error Studios

Herd It! is a cozy puzzle game about guiding a scattered flock back home. Drag your sheepdog across meadows, river valleys and forests, open fence gates, cross bridges and lead every sheep into the pen.

Features

  • Over 100 hand-made levels across themed worlds
  • Special sheep: leaders with bells, sleepy sheep, lambs, goats and more
  • Collect dogs, wacky creatures, trails and pen themes
  • Race your best times on the Google Play Games leaderboard
