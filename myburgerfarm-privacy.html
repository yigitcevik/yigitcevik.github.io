Privacy Policy

My Burger Farm · Sacred Error · Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how My Burger Farm (the "Game"), developed by Sacred Error ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the Game on your mobile device.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal contact information. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows ads. Ads may be personalized based on your device's advertising ID, depending on your consent and device settings.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the Game.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Buying "No Ads" removes forced ads. Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch may still be offered.

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases of virtual items (such as gems, boosts and permanent packs). Virtual items have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

5. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (money, levels, unlocked areas, settings, purchased items) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes this progress.

6. Data security and retention

Data sent by the services listed above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not operate our own servers that store your information. Retention of data collected by third-party services is governed by their own policies.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Because we do not collect personal contact information and do not keep player data on our own servers, there is no account to delete. To remove local game data, uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings. To manage data collected by Google or Unity, use the controls described in their privacy policies, or contact us and we will help where we can.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. You can contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new service. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
